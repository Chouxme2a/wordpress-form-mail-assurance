## Show HN

**Title:** Show HN: End-to-end Contact Form 7 email monitoring for WordPress agencies

I built WordPress Form & Mail Assurance for agencies running care plans across 20–100 client sites. It submits an isolated synthetic Contact Form 7 test through the site’s existing WordPress mail path, verifies arrival through Gmail, retries before declaring an incident, and sends a recovery notice when delivery returns.

The founding pilot supports Contact Form 7 only. There is a free public compatibility checker, and delivery is never claimed until the helper is installed and the real path passes. The full failure/retry/incident/recovery loop has been exercised against a real WordPress, SMTP and Gmail setup.

Site: https://wp-form-mail-assurance.wordpress-form-mail-assurance.workers.dev/
